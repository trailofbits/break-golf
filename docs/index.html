How it works

A challenge gives you two worlds and the type your proof must inhabit. Write an adversary, prove it tells them apart. Nothing is run — the win is the proof.

You supply
strategygiven the answers so far, the next query
verdictaccept or reject, read off the transcript
winsthe proof, at the pinned type
budget, advNum, advDenyour score, as Lean definitions the type is indexed by

Scoring

score = log₂( budget / advantage^e )

Bits of security your attack refutes; lower is better. Par is the designer's own claim.

You declare budget, advNum and advDen in your Lean and the challenge type is indexed by them — so CI reads your score out of the proof, and there is nothing to claim.
